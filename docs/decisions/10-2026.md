## Complete account imports against the refactor's new types and interfaces modules

- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Decided by:** MiniMax M3 and GPT-6 Astra, following the maintainer's app-refactor repair request
- **Context:** consumers still imported the deleted `app/src/accounts/types.ts`; the CLI failed at startup with `ERR_MODULE_NOT_FOUND`, and all 14 tests in `cli.test.ts` failed.
- **Decision:** migrate remaining consumers to `app/src/types/accounts.ts` for account primitives and `ACCOUNT_IDS`, and `app/src/interfaces/accounts.ts` for account interfaces, preserving the maintainer's split. Update the two acceptance-test consumers' type-only imports too.
- **Why:** complete the module move without restoring the deleted module or changing application behavior.
- **Consequences:** app and acceptance typechecks pass; all 14 CLI tests and 130 related account tests pass.
- **Sources:** `app/src/cli.ts`, `app/src/types/accounts.ts`, `app/src/interfaces/accounts.ts`; MiniMax M3 validation and Astra diff review in this session.
